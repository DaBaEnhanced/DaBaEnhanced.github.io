## Description

**1996, as imagined in 1996. One mind, two worlds, and a rig that won't let you come back.**

Theo Brandt is a broke arcade champion with the best hands in the city and nothing else to show for it. Then CEREBRA Systems offers to pay for his reaction times: test pilot of a neural VR rig that dives into the Deep, the network under the network. Every dive makes him more than human. Every dive makes coming back harder. And somebody else has been diving for three years, and never came back.

DEEPLINK is a high-speed 3D flight shooter through cyberspace, driven by mid-90s trance and hard techno, with the story told in black-and-white comic pages.

### The music is the level
Every dive is one track. Its tempo sets your speed, the build is a gate run on the snare roll, the drop is the **Breach**, the second drop is the **boss**, and enemies spawn and fire on the beat. Act on the beat to build Sync up to ×8. It's never a rhythm game, but it always feels like one.

### SHIFT
Every layer of the Deep has a shadow copy laid over the same corridor: the **A-side** and the **B-side**. Press SHIFT and the world flips, and the music flips with it, from airy trance to saturated hard techno, never out of time. Enemies, shots and gates belong to one world: ghosts can't touch you, your own world's shots are absorbed and power up your weapon, and bosses only bleed when you match their colour.

### Features
- 9 story dives and 21 contracts, each with its own track: 33 original tracks in all
- 9 layers in early-90s CGI, each with its own B-side: a grid city, a chrome sea, firewall canyons, a burning archive, ray-marched fractals, Juno's garden, Black ICE, and the Core collapsing into a black hole
- 8 bosses built and animated in code, from the Auditor to the Deep itself
- Lock-on volleys, rolls that deflect shots, boost, brake and a weapon that grows to a full fan of fire
- 10 neural mods that change Theo's body, his comic pages and which of the **three endings** you can reach
- Free Dive, Remix, Endless Deep, Time Attack, and Custom Music: load your own track and the game builds a dive from it
- 400×300 dithered 15-bit look, CRT filter, or a clean high-res mode
- Accessibility: remapping, auto-fire, auto-lock, wider gates, game speed, low-flash mode, colour-blind bullets, subtitles, four difficulties

### Controls
| Action | Keyboard + mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | WASD / arrows / mouse | Left stick |
| Fire | Space / left mouse / J | RT |
| Lock-on (hold, sweep, release) | Right mouse / K | LT |
| Roll | Q / E | LB / RB |
| **Shift worlds** | Shift / middle mouse | X |
| Boost / Brake | C / Ctrl | A / B |
| Neural power | F | Y |
| Pause | Esc / P | Start |

Every key can be remapped. One-key mode: the autopilot flies and fires, you tap to shift. On phones and tablets there are on-screen touch controls.

Runs in the browser on **WebGPU**: Chrome or Edge on a desktop works best.
