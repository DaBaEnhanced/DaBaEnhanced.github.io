## Description

**1999, as imagined in 1986. A memory dealer, a stolen prototype, and a girl living in the dashboard.**

Port Avalon runs on rain, neon and other people's memories. Vic Calloway sells them for a living, until a delivery goes wrong and leaves him with a cracked chip and a stolen car. He plugs the chip in, and **LYRA** wakes up in the dashboard: a mind cut into seven shards and sold across a broken continent.

Every shard you bring back gives her a memory, and gives the car a new ability.

**He rebuilt her piece by piece. Then he had to stop her.**

AFTERGLOW is an arcade combat racer in the spirit of *Lotus Esprit Turbo Challenge 2*, *Black Viper* and *OutRun*: a hand-pixelled pseudo-3D road, ramps and jumps, forks, weather, armed rivals and bosses, and a story told in 80s-OVA comic panels.

### Features
- 7 chapters, 21 stages: rain-soaked downtown, sunset coast, a desert of satellite dishes, a snowbound pass, flooded lowlands, an industrial wasteland, the city grid at dawn
- Checkpoint runs, escapes, pursuits, duels, gauntlets, jump runs and deliveries
- Front guns with heat, mines, oil, smoke and EMP, and a car special
- 7 abilities from LYRA's shards: call-outs, beat-synced Overdrive, threat radar, lock-on, traffic hack, shield and Afterglow slow-time
- Mama Dolores's garage: repairs, upgrades, tyres, paint and memory reels to keep or sell
- 6 cars, S–D ratings, Arcade, Time Trial, Endless Highway and Night Shift
- Split-screen Versus and Convoy co-op for two players
- A retrowave and late-80s club soundtrack, a car radio, and a CRT filter
