## Description

**The Empire built its city from the bones of a serpent. One skull woke up.**

VERTEBRA is a side-scrolling shoot 'em up roguelite in the spirit of Saint Dragon. You are a flying dragon skull, and **your body is your build**: every enemy you destroy can become a new vertebra chained behind you.

- **Plates** soak up bullets, **turrets** fire with your breath, **mirror scales** send shots back, **bombs** explode, **medics** heal their neighbours, **amplifiers** speed up the turret behind them, and **relics** are torn from the bosses.
- **Block** bullets with your spine, **curl** it into a shield around the skull, **whip** the tail, **shed** it as a bomb, and when the Charge is full unleash a screen-wide **Burst**.
- The order of the segments matters: rearrange them at the shrines, fuse them at the forge, buy them from smugglers.

### Features
- 6 regions, from the Foundry Coast to a secret sixth one
- 30 enemy types, 6 elites and 6 bosses
- 50+ segments and relics, with adjacency combos and bone sets
- 6 playable Skulls, each with its own breath and Burst
- Story / Standard / Arcade difficulty and 10 Heat modifiers
- Daily Run, Arcade Gauntlet and Practice modes
- Remappable controls, gamepad support, colour-blind bullet sets, CRT filter
