Saint Dragon — port

Assets and engine reconstructed from the original Amiga disk. Everything below runs from data extracted out of the disk image, not from screen captures.

Engine test The dragon and its trailing body, plus transcribed enemy behaviours, over the stage 1 parallax. Graphics 685 sprite objects, five stage backdrops with their parallax strips, and the ten palettes. Audio Both music tracks in stereo and the 17 sound effects.
