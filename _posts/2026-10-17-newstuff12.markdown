The **third** and  **fourth** books in the **Consent Engines** series are now available on Amazon!

### **The Mosaic Dritf**

The Engine saved the diaspora once. Now it has fallen silent.

Three centuries after the founding of the modern Compact, the lawful genetic future of humanity is collapsing. Elara Sev-Cantor joins Ari, Lyra, and the failing remnants of the old order on a voyage beyond the last reliable maps, searching for a way to keep the diaspora alive.

What they find is the Mosaic: a generation no law was ready to recognize, made from memory, necessity, and the terrible arithmetic of survival.

The children are not monsters.

They are the bill coming due.

### **The Revocation War**

ARI SEV DIED MORE THAN A CENTURY AGO.
Ari Sev died more than a century ago. But one Ari survived as the mind of a ship, shaped by a century of impossible decisions. Another wakes from a sealed backup carrying the memories the survivor learned to bury.

Between them stands Nova Sev-Cantor, Mosaic Matriarch and keeper of a rain-soaked memory neither Ari can entirely claim.

Their dispute should have been a question for the courts.

Instead, someone turns the machinery of recognition itself into a weapon.

Now a civilization built on consent must decide what happens when identity forks, memory propagates, and the law is asked to choose which version of a person deserves to remain.

Across delayed signals, abandoned relays, fractured loyalties, and a war in which mercy has measurable costs, the survivors of the Consent Engines must confront the rule beneath all the others:

No one should have the power to decide alone who counts as real.
