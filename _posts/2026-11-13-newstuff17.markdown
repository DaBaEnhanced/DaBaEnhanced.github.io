My seventh game, **Ark 02: The Losts**, is out! It is a turn-based squad survival game with RPG elements, played across isometric decks, and it is based on my Ark novels.

Four colonists grown in pods and raised in a dream wake up for the first time inside Ark 02, a generation ship hollowed out of an asteroid that has drifted for a million years. Their only guide is **MEND**, a battered maintenance drone that speaks in a nursery voice. Nine thousand sleepers are still dreaming. Something in the walls is not.

What's in it:
- real-time exploration that turns into turn-based combat the moment something hostile shows up, with action points for every step, shot and door;
- 29 creature types in five families, each hunting by its own sense: heat, noise, vibration, sight, rhythm or scent. And they learn: kill everything the same way and the next sector comes ready for it;
- cover, flanking, shots from above, overwatch, harpoon pins, strobes, foam, nitrogen, fire, door crushes and braced doors;
- bodies instead of hit points: wounds by body part, bleeding, broken limbs, infection, cold, hypoxia, stress and panic;
- MEND, who crawls the ducts, scouts ahead, carries your spare gear and remembers how you treat it;
- 28 sectors across four acts, two bases, crafting, a ship map, more sleepers to wake and three endings;
- puzzles explained somewhere in the ark itself, in logs, terminals, graffiti and tactile glyphs;
- an HD pack with dynamic lighting, an optional CRT filter, remappable keys and gamepad support.
