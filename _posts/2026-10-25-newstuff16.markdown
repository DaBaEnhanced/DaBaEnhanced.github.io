My sixth game, **AFTERGLOW**, is out! It is an arcade combat racer with a story, and a love letter to *Lotus Esprit Turbo Challenge 2*, *Black Viper* and late-night anime tapes: 1999, as imagined in 1986.

Port Avalon runs on rain, neon and other people's memories. Vic Calloway sells them for a living, until a delivery goes wrong and leaves him with a cracked chip and a stolen prototype. He plugs the chip into the dashboard, and a girl wakes up in the car: **LYRA**, a mind cut into seven shards and sold across a broken continent. Every shard you bring back gives her a memory and gives the car a new ability. He rebuilt her piece by piece. Then he had to stop her.

What's in it:
- seven chapters and 21 stages, from rain-soaked downtown to the sunset coast, a desert of satellite dishes, a snowbound pass, the flooded lowlands, an industrial wasteland and the city grid at dawn;
- ramps and jumps, OutRun-style forks, tunnels, toll gates, trains and weather;
- guns that overheat, mines, oil, smoke and EMP, bikers, gun vans, interceptors, drones and a gunship;
- seven abilities from LYRA's shards, including Overdrive, which rewards turbo and gear shifts on the beat of the music;
- Mama Dolores's garage, six cars, memory reels to keep or sell, and the story told in 80s-OVA comic panels;
- Arcade, Time Trial, Endless Highway, Night Shift, and split-screen Versus and Convoy for two players;
- a retrowave and late-80s club soundtrack, a car radio and, of course, a CRT filter.

It runs in the browser: Chrome or Edge on a desktop works best (WebGPU, with a WebGL 2 fallback), and it also plays on phones and tablets, with on-screen touch controls.
